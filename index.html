PENDULUM GAME

A chaotic physics challenge! Aim your launch, dodge the hazards, and master the chaos.

How to Play: Adjust initial parameters and launch the pendulum to destroy all targets (Red) within the time limit!
  • Aim & Launch: Adjust sliders, drag the tip bob directly, or press Spacebar to FIRE.
  • Mid-Air PULSE: Tap the canvas or press Spacebar mid-flight to trigger an explosive shockwave and steer the tip.
  • Target Clearance: Destroy every red target before the countdown timer reaches zero to clear the stage.
  • Progression: Each Stage introduces new physical elements (Bombs, Rails, Black Holes, Winds). Watch for stage briefings!
